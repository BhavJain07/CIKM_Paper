\section{Step 1 --- EasyChair abstract (May 30)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \url{https://easychair.org/my/conference?conf=cikm26}
\item New submission $\rightarrow$ \textbf{CIKM 2026 Resource}
\item Title and abstract from \texttt{paper/abstract\_easychair.txt}
\item Real author names and affiliations (single-blind; names go on the PDF too)
\item Nominate $\geq$1 author as reviewer
\end{enumerate}

\section{Step 2 --- Edit paper (before Overleaf)}

In \texttt{paper/main.tex} replace:
\begin{itemize}
\item Author names, affiliations, emails
\item GitHub URL (\texttt{YOUR\_ORG/ProvBench-RAG})
\item Zenodo DOI after upload (\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX})
\end{itemize}

\section{Step 3 --- Overleaf PDF (by June 6)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Run \texttt{bash scripts/package\_overleaf.sh}
\item Upload \texttt{paper/overleaf\_bundle/} to Overleaf
\item Main file: \texttt{main.tex}
\item Compile pdfLaTeX + BibTeX
\item Check: $\leq$4 pages of content (appendix counts toward 4); references extra
\item Download PDF
\end{enumerate}

\section{Step 4 --- Zenodo DOI}

Follow \texttt{ZENODO.md}. Resource track expects a citable archive. Add DOI to paper before camera-ready; June submission may say ``pending'' if upload is in progress.

\section{Step 5 --- Upload paper (June 6)}

Same EasyChair submission $\rightarrow$ upload Resource PDF.
